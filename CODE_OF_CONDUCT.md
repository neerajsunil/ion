# Code of Conduct

Ion follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on
the work. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

## Reporting

reports are reviewed and kept confidential.
